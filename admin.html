Security & Registration Authorization Policy
  • Only emails saved in this whitelist are eligible to create an account on the IGYR portal.
  • Adding an email automatically dispatches an official invitation with link https://demoigyr.vercel.app.
  • Important: Adding an email does not mean the institute is officially registered. The institution must submit registration on the portal, which will then appear under "New Registrations" for final Super Admin review and verification.
  • If an unauthorized email attempts to register, the system allows up to 2 attempts. On the 3rd unauthorized attempt, account creation is locked out for 5 minutes for security.
